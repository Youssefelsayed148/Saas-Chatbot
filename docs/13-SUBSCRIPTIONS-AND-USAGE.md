# Usage and billing
MVP: internal plan labels and enforceable limits for bots, pages, ingestion runs, monthly messages/tokens, seats (agents), contacts, channel connections and sentiment runs; transactional usage events with idempotency. Billing checkout is explicitly deferred pending provider approval for merchant country, currency, recurring support and fees. Do not invent prices or payment vendor. Later billing: webhook signature verification, replay protection, cancellation/grace state, invoice reconciliation, owner-only billing actions. Many keeps its own subscription; the SaaS only counts messages and connections on its side. No shared entitlements.
